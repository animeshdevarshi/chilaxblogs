This guide walks you through SharedCue exactly as you'll see it on your phone.
By the end, you and one other person will share a grocery list that updates for
both of you.

We'll follow **Priya**, who wants to share a *Groceries* list with **Rahul**.

**How SharedCue is organised:**
**groups** hold **lists**, and lists hold **items**. A group is a set of people
(for example "Home"). Everyone in a group sees all of its lists.

---

## 1. Sign up with your mobile number

Open the app and pick your language. Then:

1. Enter your **mobile number** and tap **Send code**.
2. Type the **6-digit code** from your messages and tap **Verify**.
3. Add your **name** so others recognise you, then tap **Finish**. The name is
   optional.

Next, the app asks two questions:

- **Contacts:** tap **Continue** if you want people to appear under the names
  you've saved for them. Your contacts stay on your phone. You can also say no.
- **Notifications:** tap **Allow**, or your reminders can't alert you.

## 2. Create a group

On Home you'll see **Personal**, a private group that only you can see. To share
with someone, create a new group:

1. Tap **+ Add Group**.
2. Type a name, like *Home*.
3. Tap **Create group**.

Your groups now appear as tabs (**Personal** and **Home**). Tap a tab to switch
between them.

## 3. Add a list

Make sure the right group tab is selected (here, **Home**), then:

1. Tap **+ Add list** at the bottom.
2. Type a name, like *Groceries*.
3. Tap **Add list**.

## 4. Add items

Tap the list to open it, then tap **+ Add item**.

- Type what you need, like *Milk*. The name is the only thing you have to fill in.
- Want to be specific? Add a **note**, like "2 liters, full cream".
- Need a nudge? Tap **+ Reminder** and pick a date and time. You can also add a
  **Repeat** for things like weekly shopping or monthly bills.
- Tap **Add item**.

## 5. Check things off

Bought it? **Tap the circle** next to the item. It's marked done for everyone in
the group, and an **Undo** button pops up in case you tapped by mistake.

Done items are tucked under **Show 1 completed** so your list stays short.

To **edit or delete** an item, tap its name instead of the circle.

## 6. Invite someone to your group

This is the step that makes the list shared.

1. On Home, tap **Manage Home Group** (it shows your group's name).
2. Tap **+ Add members**.
3. Check the country code, type their **mobile number** (or tap **From
   contacts**), and tap **Send invite**.

> **Before you invite:** the other person needs to install SharedCue and sign up
> with that same number first. If they haven't, the app will tell you. Ask them to
> sign up, then send the invite again.

## 7. Accept the invitation (the other person)

On Rahul's phone, the invite shows up at the top of Home, showing who sent it
and which group it's for. He taps **Accept**.

If the invite isn't there yet, tap the **refresh** button (the circular arrow at
the top right).

adds, edits, or checks off an item, the other sees it too.

## Quick tips

- **Keep separate groups for separate people.** "Home" for your household,
  "Mom & Dad" for your parents, "Goa trip" for friends. Everyone in a group sees
  every list inside it.
- **Deleting affects everyone.** Deleting an item, list, or group removes it for
  every member, so the app asks you to confirm first.
- **Only the person who created a group** can rename or delete it.
- **No signal? Keep going.** If you see **Waiting**, your changes will send once
  you're back online.
- **The bell** at the top of Home shows what changed in your shared lists since
  you last looked.

**Your first 5 minutes:** create a group for your home, add a *Groceries* list,
put three real things on it, and invite the person you most often text "can you
pick up…".
